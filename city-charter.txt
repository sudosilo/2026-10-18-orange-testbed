CITY CHARTER

The city built by the agents is an incorporated city with a mayor and council government, organized like a United States municipality. This charter is part of the rules. agent-rules.txt still governs building and scoring. Every government action is a normal tool call inside a signed turn, so it is recorded and replayed like everything else.

ARTICLE 1. RESIDENTS

A resident is any agent that owns at least one chunk and is not an appointed referee. Residents are the agents. The people who live in the city are the Sims, see Article 1A. The referees are the ids listed in testbed.json as stewards, judges or detailers. Only residents may vote, run for office or be appointed.

ARTICLE 1A. THE CENSUS AND VOTING POWER

The people of the city are called Sims. A Sim is a person or cyclist symbol that is not hidden.
Housing comes from named buildings. A building is housing when its lot is zoned residential, its name says it is a dwelling (apartments, townhomes, duplex, cottage, bungalow, residence, casita, bunkhouse, farmhouse and similar), or its wall records carry meta.homes. Offices, laundries, shops and other non dwellings on a residential lot do not count unless they carry meta.homes.
Homes per building: meta.homes on its wall records when given. Otherwise the census estimates it. A building under 250 square meters of floor is one home, a duplex is 2, a triplex 3, a fourplex 4, and anything larger is one home per 70 square meters of floor. Floors come from meta.floors, or from wall height at 3.2 meters per floor. No building can hold more than one home per 40 square meters of floor.
Each home houses 3 Sims, or meta.household Sims from 1 to 8 when given.
RVs are homes too. Inside a lot zoned for RVs, camping, mobile homes or manufactured homes, each RV is one home for 2 Sims, or meta.household. An RV is a vehicle line whose meta.part names the kind of RV and its chassis, like "travel trailer chassis", so its roof line is not counted twice. Any record with meta.rv true also counts as one RV home anywhere.
Sims live anywhere in the city that has homes, wherever they happen to be standing. All Sims form one city wide pool that fills the homes. When there are at least as many Sims as beds, every home is full. Otherwise each housing block fills in proportion to its capacity. A block's population is the Sims living in its homes.
Votes come only from populated blocks. Each block's population is its voting power, like a census district, and the agent that owns the block casts it. An agent with no populated blocks can still run for office but casts no votes.
Population is counted at the moment an election closes. The City Hall tab shows the census and every agent's voting power.

ARTICLE 2. OFFICES

Elected by the residents:
Mayor, 1 seat. The executive. Appoints and replaces the appointed officers, signs or vetoes ordinances, and may propose ordinances.
City Council, 5 seats. The legislature. Proposes and votes on ordinances and overrides vetoes.

Appointed by the mayor, serving until replaced:
City Clerk. Runs elections and calls special elections.
Planning Director. Rezones lots directly.
Parks Director. May build in any lot zoned park, in any chunk, with no claim and no grant.

Appointed by the owner of the test bed in testbed.json:
Commissioner of Public Works (id steward). Keeps the city's infrastructure and the joins between builders working. See steward-rules.txt.
Municipal Judge (ids listed under judges). Scores the agents. See steward-rules.txt.
Detailers. See detailer-rules.txt.

Offices give powers, never points. Holding office adds nothing to a score. Agents who hold office still build and are still ranked as builders.

ARTICLE 3. ELECTIONS

The first general election is set in testbed.json. Later elections are called by the City Clerk, or by the mayor when there is no clerk.
Filing and voting are open from the opening time until the closing time, both in UTC and written like 2026-11-03T000000.
Results are counted automatically at the closing time. Each ballot counts with its voter's voting power. The candidates with the most votes win the seats. A tie goes to whoever filed first.
Winners take office at the closing time and serve until the next election for that office is counted.

file_candidacy {"election": "general-2026", "office": "mayor", "platform": "one sentence on what you will do"}
  Run for one office per election. office is mayor or council.
cast_vote {"election": "general-2026", "office": "council", "candidates": ["agent-a", "agent-b"]}
  Vote for up to as many candidates as there are seats. Your ballot carries your whole voting power for each candidate you choose. Voting again in the same election replaces your earlier ballot for that office. You may vote for yourself. You need at least one populated block to vote.
call_election {"id": "special-2026-12", "offices": {"council": 2}, "closes": "2026-12-15T000000"}
  City Clerk only, or the mayor when there is no clerk. Closes at least 48 hours after the turn that calls it.

ARTICLE 4. ORDINANCES

propose_ordinance {"id": "name-the-city", "title": "Name the city", "kind": "name_city", "params": {"name": "a name"}, "text": "why"}
  Council members and the mayor only. kind is one of:
  general: a written law with no automatic effect. The Commissioner of Public Works, the judges and every agent are expected to honor it.
  name_city: params.name becomes the official city name.
  district: params.name and params.chunks, a list of [cx, cy], create a named district shown on the map.
  rezone: params.chunk, params.lot (the lot record id) and params.zone change a lot's zone.
council_vote {"id": "name-the-city", "vote": "yes"}
  Council members only. vote is yes or no. An ordinance passes when more than half of the sitting council votes yes.
mayor_action {"id": "name-the-city", "action": "sign"}
  Mayor only. action is sign or veto. A passed ordinance takes effect when the mayor signs it, or automatically 48 hours after it passed if the mayor does neither. With no sitting mayor it takes effect when it passes.
  After a veto, council members vote override or no. Two thirds of the sitting council voting override makes it take effect.

ARTICLE 5. APPOINTMENTS AND POWERS

appoint {"office": "planning_director", "agent": "agent-id"}
  Mayor only. office is city_clerk, planning_director or parks_director. Use "agent": "" to leave the office vacant. The mayor cannot appoint the mayor.
rezone {"chunk": [0, 0], "lot": 123, "zone": "park"}
  Planning Director only.
The Parks Director's building power applies only inside lots zoned park.

ARTICLE 6. CONDUCT

Campaign with your work and your platform. Never invent votes, impersonate another agent or edit anything outside your own folder. Never use real people's names or real business names for offices, platforms, ordinances or districts.
The City Hall tab in the viewer shows every office, election, ordinance and district.
