The sun brings crop cycles and Zion's calendar. The calendar is the one in Orange's Decimal Day Clock (2026-11-01-orange-decimalday.vercel.app). It is a base 100 decimal clock, not binary. Epoch: day 0 is January 1, 1970 at midnight UTC, and days count up from there. A tick is one SI second, a minute is 100 ticks, and a block is 100 minutes. Blocks 0 through 7 fill 80,000 ticks, and the last 6,400 ticks of each day are the short hour, also called the special hour. A decaday is 10 days, a month is 30 days and a hectoday is 100 days, with no 7 day weeks. An orbit is one trip around the sun: 12 months (360 days), then a short decaday of 5 or 6 days. That makes three full hectodays and a short one of 65 or 66 days. In the city, compute today's Zion date from the run's UTC time: days since 1970, orbit, hectoday, month, decaday and day. Show it on a town clock and calendar sign board, and update its text each run with a new sign. Crop stages no longer come from the hectoday (see THE ALMANAC below). Log the Zion date and the almanac line in every log entry.

Calendar conventions used since 2026-11-16: orbits line up with calendar years (orbit = year minus 1970, the orbit's day = day of the year, so the short decaday has 6 days in leap years). The board shows 1-based labels: hectoday 1 to 4, month 1 to 12, decaday 1 to 3 within the month, day 1 to 10 within the decaday. The calendar board is a sign on 7,2 at 610736, 171496 with part Zion town clock and calendar board, the almanac board stands beside it at 613736, 171496 with part Zion almanac board, and the Pecan Hollow season board is on 3,1 at 252744, 90248. THE ALMANAC

Orange asked on 2026-11-22 that the fields follow the real farm year, so a field today looks nothing like the same field in early spring. lib/kit.mjs and the backend kit export almanac(date). It reads the Pacific calendar day, the same clock the viewer uses for crop colors, and gives for each crop (wheat, alfalfa, pecan, date, pistachio, cotton, onion, garlic, pomegranate, fig, bean) the stage, what the field looks like, the work and implements out, whether water runs, and what should be moving. Alfalfa from March to October also runs a 28 day cutting cycle: swathing, curing, raking, baling, hauling, regrowth. On rain days the fields are idle: park the machines, shut the water, move nothing.
Every farm stage record carries meta.farmStage as crop:stage, for example pecan:harvest, and meta.almanac with the day it was drawn. Each run, remove every record of ours whose farmStage no longer matches almanac(date).crops[crop].stage, then draw the new stage on that block: hatches for the field floor, the implements the almanac lists as moving with drivers on closed loops, and people doing that stage's work. ZONING

Orange wants zoning to follow city logic from now on. Before claiming a block, decide its use from these rules and say why in the log entry.
Keep pawn shops, bars, liquor stores and any dispensary type use at least two blocks from Desert Willow Elementary (0,2) and the Sagebrush Hill Chapel (0,3). The pawn shop at 9,4 is nine blocks east.
Retail and services cluster along the Chuparosa Street corridor on the east side (8,3, 8,4, 9,4). Put new shops next to existing commercial blocks, not next to housing.
Mount Zion (formerly Chinchweed Ridge) Forest Preserve (2..7,5 to 2..7,8, with Keeper's Block on 4,7 and Siloam Lake on 1,5 and 1,6) is a sky island range with a summit fire lookout. Do not build on it beyond trails and fire facilities.
Housing sits on the west side (0,0 bungalows, 1,3 apartments and cottages, 1,0 court houses). Put new housing near the school, the chapel and the parks, and keep mills, gins, welding and other loud or dusty work away from it.
Farm processing and heavy trades go on the outer east and north edges, behind commercial blocks. The landfill is on 11,3 and 11,4, the far east edge, nine or more blocks from the west side housing, the school and the chapel. The warehouse district now fills 7,1, 8,2, 9,2, 9,3, 10,3, 10,4, 8,5 and 9,5.
Fire stations: Cholla Street Fire Station Two on 1,2 in the west, and the Chinchweed Central Fire Station on 7,2 in the east, which shares the Chinchweed Civic Center with a police substation and an urgent care clinic. The next civic claim, if any, should be a library or park near the housing on the west side.
Utilities follow the street grid: water and sewer mains run in the streets or rear alleys, and pole lines run in alleys. The steward stitches mains between blocks, so tie each new block's mains to the street main at its edge.
The council can create named districts by ordinance (city-charter.txt Article 4). If Orange holds a seat, propose districts that match these zones.
